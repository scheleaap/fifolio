1 · Introduction and goals

Why Fifolio exists, the three to five qualities it must not compromise on, and who has a stake in it.

1.1 Requirements overview

The problem

German income tax computes gains and losses on securities under the FIFO principle, per account (Depot). German brokers do this for their customers; brokers outside Germany do not, so the taxpayer must. Fifolio does it for accounts at Saxo Bank NL, and also for Trade Republic accounts, which serves as an independent check on figures a German broker already reported. domain.md › Background

What the system does

For each account, it determines which acquisitions a disposal consumes under FIFO, and the resulting raw gain or loss in EUR. Applying tax law to those figures is the user's job. DOM-001 DEC-001

Corporate actions are in scope

Splits, cash mergers, share-class exchanges, bond redemptions and tender offers dominate the real exports rather than sitting at their edges. Ignoring them would make holdings and cost bases wrong. They are modeled as transaction variants, not as a separate entity. domain.md › Scope DEC-024

Explicitly out of scope (v1)

  • All tax law applied on top of the raw figures: Teilfreistellung, loss pots, Vorabpauschale, Sparer-Pauschbetrag, the taxes themselves. DOM-001
  • Income that is not a disposal: cash dividends, interest, deposits, withdrawals, fees. These are recognized at import and deliberately not stored. A dividend paid in shares is the exception. DOM-002 DOM-124
  • Gains on foreign currency cash balances.
  • Network exposure, multiple users, authentication. ARC-022

Known limitations the design accepts (accrued interest, averaged transferred parcels, lost Altbestand status, and others) are listed in domain.md › Known limitations.

1.2 Quality goals

The five qualities with the highest priority, most important first. Each has one scenario that makes it concrete. The full set of quality requirements belongs in section 10.

The specification does not name quality goals explicitly. These are inferred from its stated reasons, most directly from testing.md: "A wrong cost basis produces a plausible number in a tax return and stays wrong for years." They need the owner's confirmation.

#GoalISO 25010Scenario
1 Correct figures
Every gain, cost and fee is right to the cent, and every total reconciles with its rows.		 Functional correctness Buy 10 at 100, sell 5, then a 2:1 split, then sell the rest. The first sale's cost basis is 500, not 250, and the allocation shares sum exactly to their parent figures. DOM-103 DOM-061
2 Fail safe, never guess
When information is missing or unknown, the system stops and names the problem. It never produces a plausible wrong number.		 Safety (fail safe) A Trade Republic file contains a corporate action type the specification does not know. The whole file is rejected, naming the type, the row and the transaction id, and nothing is imported. IMP-TR-010 DEC-019
A sale exceeds the known holdings: attribution is blocked and the shortfall is named. DOM-057
3 Auditable
Every figure can be traced back to the broker rows and the user input it rests on.		 Accountability A reviewer picks one row of the income tax report. They can follow it to its allocations, the transactions behind them, the verbatim source rows, and the FX rate and rate date used. DOM-007 DOM-016 DOM-028 DOM-034
4 Reproducible and recoverable
The same inputs always give the same state, and the state can be rebuilt.		 Recoverability The user undoes an import and imports the same file again. The account returns exactly to where it was, including transactions completed by hand. DOM-108 TST-010
After losing the database, the user re-imports the exports and replays the manual-information file, without redoing any research. CLI-037
5 Confidential
Personal financial data never leaves the user's machine.		 Confidentiality The server binds only 127.0.0.1. The real exports are gitignored, and CI runs on anonymized fixtures. ARC-022 DEC-017

Deliberately not among the goals: performance (a few hundred rows a year), availability (a local tool started on demand), and portability beyond a Rust toolchain.
